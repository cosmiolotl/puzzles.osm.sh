Black to move.
White has played Re2, a passive move.
The position is a lot more pressuring than white realizes. Why?

## Explanation

A passive defense of the b2 pawn allows black to advance their queenside pawn majority. Much better would be 1. Bc2, allowing for 1. ...Qxb2 2. Rb1 winning back the lost pawn and creating counterplay for white.

3. ...Qc4 ties the queen to the defense of the white rook, making black able to then play ...cxb2 if allowed (4. bxc3 ...Nxc3 creates a fork, 5. Re1 Nxb1 6. Rxb1 Qe4+ wins the rook).

With these moves, white is too slow to create counterplay on the kingside and unable to stop the advancing pawns, and black later went on to win this game.
